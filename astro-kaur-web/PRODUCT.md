# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- The owner administers astrology reading bookings, calls clients about text readings, manages voice call availability, and maintains website content.
- Customers purchase text readings or voice call readings and review their bookings and receipts.

## Product Purpose

The Astro Kaur website lets customers book astrology readings and gives the owner tools to manage those readings and the site's public content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Text readings are paid orders that the owner follows up on personally when available; they have no appointment or calendar schedule.
- Voice call readings are paid before customers choose an available call time.
- Voice availability and appointments are managed separately from text readings.
- The admin workspace manages bookings, customer records, free reading requests, testimonials, and website copy.
- Preserve the existing booking, payment, email, content, and admin access flows when changing the admin interface.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve The Astro Kaur name and the website's existing light and dark theme preference.

- Existing customer records and website content editors: `src/app/account/` and `src/app/admin/`.
