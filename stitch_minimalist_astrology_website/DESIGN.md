## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes deep, ancient Vedic wisdom with contemporary analytical clarity. Built for modern seekers, professionals, and mindful individuals, the system deliberately avoids mystical clichés, dramatic sensationalism, and high-noise commercialism. Instead, it balances high-end editorial calm (inspired by Sarvam AI's structural precision, whisper-thin micro-borders, and generous whitespace) with effortless clarity in service discovery and clear pricing cues.

The design movement is **Minimalist Editorial with Celestial Craft**:
- **Tone & Mood**: Calm, cerebral, empowering, and reverent. Grounded in self-awareness, personal growth, and conscious decision-making rather than fatalistic prediction.
- **Visual Structure**: Architectural whitespace, crisp typography contrasts, fine hairline strokes (0.5px to 1px), and delicate celestial geometry (thin astrological rings, lunar arcs, and subtle mandala motifs).
- **Surface Texture**: Tactile warmth drawn from cream/ivory canvases with midnight slate contrast panels that frame the closing narrative.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of tranquility and elevated luxury by replacing stark pure whites with warm ivory and parchment tones. 

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Midnight slate provides razor-sharp readability for editorial headlines and establishes grounding contrast in deep evening closing panels.
- **Secondary (`#E5B842` / `#F5D061`)**: Butter-gold and sun-gold serve as the dynamic focus layer. Reserved for primary conversion triggers, focal badges, celestial highlights, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#6B3448` & `#B4533C`)**: Muted plum and terracotta are used contextually for subtle tags, icons, and micro-accents to communicate warmth, feminine energy, and deep contemplative heritage.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` / `#F7F2E9`)**: The tactile paper-cream backdrop that makes typography feel published rather than merely rendered.

### Contrast & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base**: Use `canvas-cream` (`#FAF8F5`) for the overarching body. Section alternations transition into `surface-cream-subtle` (`#F7F2E9`).
- **Dark Inversion Block**: Pre-footer closures and high-impact focal cards invert to `midnight-slate` (`#0F172A`) paired with `butter-gold` text highlights and translucent celestial glow backdrops.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Never use heavy gray lines. Use `border-delicate` (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) or tinted `border-gold-subtle` (`rgba(198, 166, 107, 0.3)`) to maintain an airy, high-craft finish.

## Typography

The typography pairs an authoritative, high-contrast serif (`Playfair Display`) with an ultra-clean, modern geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). This juxtaposition anchors the system's identity: ancient heritage rendered with contemporary precision.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`)**: Used strictly for hero statements, section headings, quote highlights, and pricing callouts. Large headlines feature tight negative letter-spacing for refined, magazine-grade elegance.
- **Interface & Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers immaculate legibility across body copy, analytical narrative descriptions, micro-labels, pricing metadata, and interactive controls.
- **Eyebrow Headers**: Always uppercase with generous tracking (`0.14em`), rendered in muted antique gold or plum to lead readers into content without shouting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial 12-column grid system with a maximum content container width of `1240px`. Whitespace is deployed with deliberate abundance to create breathing room, clarity, and an unhurried reading cadence.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column structure with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic outer margins up to `4rem`. Sections alternate with generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters. Service cards shift to a 2-column or controlled horizontal carousel.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen edges. Section margins contract to `margin-mobile` to maintain content proportion.
- **The Service Carousel**: Uses a unified snap-scroll rail (`overflow-x: auto` with CSS scroll-snapping) on mobile and tablet, expanding to an aligned grid or bounded 3-card presentation on larger desktop screens.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows or glossy skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved through **tonal stratification, hairline borders, and ambient sun-tinted glows**:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Baseline canvas in `canvas-cream` (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Surfaces layered on `surface-cream-subtle` with a crisp `0.5px` or `1px` border of `border-delicate`. Shadow is faint and diffuse: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus)**: The card elevates subtly with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, and the boundary border transitions to `border-gold-subtle`.
- **Level 3 (Celestial Glows & Modals)**: Used for high-emphasis callouts, popovers, and free-reading banners. Uses an ambient golden halo: `0 0 40px rgba(245, 208, 97, 0.15)` paired with a soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Night Surface (Pre-Footer & Dark Mode Blocks)**: High visual contrast utilizing `midnight-slate` as the floor, with inner elements housed on `midnight-card` delineated by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances clean architectural rectangles with sacred celestial curves:

- **Border Radius (`roundedness: 2`)**: Interactive inputs, cards, and dropdowns share an `8px` (`0.5rem`) base corner radius, transitioning to `16px` (`1rem`) on outer card structures and modal containers.
- **Pill Forms**: Buttons, tag badges, and status pills adopt full continuous radii (`9999px`) to create a smooth, approachable touchpoint against sharp editorial typography.
- **Celestial Portrait Archetype**: The astrologer's featured photography is framed within an elegant **arch/oval contour** (e.g. `border-radius: 240px 240px 16px 16px` or a dedicated geometric oval), haloed by fine concentric celestial orbit rings rendered in antique gold.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Butter Gold")**: Solid `#F5D061` background with `#0F172A` bold text. Fully rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#E5B842` with subtle forward micro-translation (`translateX(2px)`) on trailing arrow icons.
- **Secondary CTA (Editorial Outline)**: Transparent background with a `1px` border of `#0F172A` (or `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)` in night mode). Pill shape, smooth hover state filling with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary / Inline Link**: Crisp body font weight with an animated bottom hairline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Service Reading Cards
- **Construction**: Clean ivory or white background enclosed with an ultra-thin `1px` border (`border-delicate`). Padded with `space-lg`.
- **Header Slot**: Category icon glyph or zodiac symbol centered in a small muted plum/gold disc (`36px`), followed by reading title in serif `headline-sm`.
- **Tag Badges**: Pill-shaped badges communicating reading format:
  - *Voice Call (30 min / 45 min)*: Muted plum background tint (`rgba(107, 52, 72, 0.08)`) with plum text.
  - *Text-based*: Subtle sage/gold tint with crisp dark label text.
- **Pricing & Action**: Clear price cue in `Playfair Display` bold (`€20`, `€40`, `€60`) anchored to bottom left, paired with an aligned "View Reading →" button or icon trigger.

### Free Reading Highlight Banner
- Positioned immediately beneath the 6 service cards.
- Elevated with a soft linear gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FAF8F5, #F7F2E9)`) framed with `border-gold-subtle`.
- Houses an ambient astrological gift icon, persuasive clarity copy, and a distinctive standalone gold action button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background filled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on cream surfaces.
- Border is `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. On focus, shifts to `#C6A66B` with a `2px` faint outer focus-ring in gold (`rgba(198, 166, 107, 0.25)`).
- Labels positioned above inputs in `label-sm` with tight vertical spacing (`0.375rem`).

### Astrology Glyphs & Micro-Embellishments
- Custom astrological glyphs inherit `currentColor` and maintain optical alignment with adjacent typography.
- Decorative astrology charts and orbit rings operate as low-opacity background watermarks (`opacity: 0.1` to `0.2`) to prevent interference with text legibility.
