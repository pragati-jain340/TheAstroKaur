## 9. Content and Implementation Notes

- Use the supplied portrait only with the astrologer’s approval.
- Keep the tone calm, personal, empowering, and grounded in the supplied copy.
- Preserve the distinction between astrology as a tool for self-awareness and guaranteed prediction.
- Do not invent qualifications, client testimonials, reviews, or outcomes.
- Use the six confirmed services and their stated prices, formats, and durations.
- Link service cards to the Services page; do not create a separate booking page.
- Include a visible Free Reading CTA directly below the service cards. Its destination can be the Free Reading page/section, while its offer and functionality remain undecided.
- Payment, appointment booking, text-reading fulfilment, and Free Reading functionality remain out of scope until separately decided.
- The About Me and My Vision text may appear in full as written here. If the final design feels too long, use shorter homepage excerpts and retain the full copy on the About page.
